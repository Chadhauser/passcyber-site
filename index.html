What it covers

Five controls. That's the whole standard.

Cyber Essentials checks five practical things about how your business runs its computers. Most owner-managed businesses are closer than they think — usually two or three small fixes away.

01

Firewalls

Your router and devices block traffic they shouldn't let in.

02

Secure setup

Unused accounts and software removed; devices lock themselves.

03

Access & MFA

People have only the access they need, with two-step login on.

04

Malware

Protection running on every device against viruses and bad software.

05

Updates

Security updates applied quickly — within 14 days of release.

The two things that fail people

Most failures come down to two traps.

Under the current question set, two things fail a Cyber Essentials submission outright — no matter how good everything else is. A cloud service (email, Microsoft, Google, accounting, banking) left without two-step login. And security updates not applied within 14 days. Our readiness check flags both before they cost you the certificate. Read the full list of what fails an application.
