PassCyber / Security questionnaires

A customer sent you a security questionnaire. We fill it in.

Before they sign or renew, many customers and prime contractors send suppliers a list of security questions. We complete it for you from your Cyber Essentials answers, you check it, and you send it back. Fixed price, £295 plus VAT.

What you get

  • Every question answered in plain, accurate English, matching how your business actually works.
  • A short list of anything you cannot honestly say yes to yet, and what it would take to fix.
  • Returned within 5 working days of receiving the questionnaire and your answers.

How it works

  1. Send us the questionnaire using the form below, or email it to hello@passcyber.co.uk.
  2. We send a fixed quote and our engagement letter. Payment is up front.
  3. We draft every answer from your Cyber Essentials answers and ask you about anything they do not cover.
  4. You check the answers and send the questionnaire to your customer.
