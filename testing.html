Penetration testing

An authorised, simulated attack on your website, office network or cloud services, to find weaknesses before someone else does. Customers, insurers and tenders increasingly ask for one each year. Testing is carried out by CREST-accredited testers, and by CHECK-certified testers where public-sector or defence work needs it.

Cyber Essentials Plus

The hands-on version of Cyber Essentials: an assessor checks a sample of your devices and accounts against the five controls. It is carried out by an IASME-licensed Certification Body. We get you ready first, so it passes, then arrange and manage the audit.

How it works

  1. You tell us what you need and why (a contract, an insurer, your own peace of mind).
  2. We agree the scope and send a fixed quote.
  3. You sign our authorisation to test. Nothing is tested without it.
  4. The test or audit is carried out, and you get the report with our fix list.

What it costs

Every test is quoted to its scope, as a fixed price before work starts. A small external test is typically a couple of days of testing; a web application usually takes longer. Your quote shows exactly what is covered.
