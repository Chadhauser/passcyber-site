Am I ready for DCC Level 0?

Ten questions. Eight come from the MOD's Level 0 Supplier Assurance Questionnaire published on GOV.UK. The other two check your Cyber Essentials scope, a common reason a Level 0 application stalls.

Is it compulsory? The Ministry of Defence has asked all its suppliers to reach Defence Cyber Certification Level 0 by 31 December 2026. That is a request from the MOD, not a legal requirement. It only becomes binding if your contract says so, but primes are already asking their subcontractors for it.
