PassCyber / IASME Cyber Assurance

IASME Cyber Assurance, without writing the paperwork yourself.

Cyber Assurance checks that security is managed, not just switched on: risk assessment, policy, data protection, backups, incidents and continuity. Most of the work is documents. We write them from how your business actually runs, and draft every answer for you to check and sign.

What it involves

You need a current Cyber Essentials certificate first, with at least a month left to run. Level 1 is a verified self-assessment: you answer the question set online, a board member signs that the answers are true, and an assessor marks them. Three gaps fail an application outright: no risk assessment, no information security policy, and no business continuity plan. Level 2 adds an audit, on site or remote, where the assessor checks your evidence and talks to your people; a moderator makes the final decision. Level 2 lasts three years, with Cyber Essentials and Level 1 renewed each year.

What we do

  • Information security and data protection policy, written for your business and its size
  • Risk assessment with named owners and a treatment plan
  • Asset register for devices and information, and a record of your personal data
  • Business continuity and disaster recovery plan, with a simple yearly test
  • Backup, incident and supplier procedures
  • Every Level 1 answer drafted, for you to check and submit
  • For Level 2: a mock audit against the standard and a fix list before the real one

The certificate itself is awarded through an IASME-accredited certification body, not by PassCyber.

What it costs

The IASME Level 1 assessment fee is set by IASME by organisation size: £320 for under 10 people, £440 for 10 to 49, £500 for 50 to 249 and £600 for 250 or more, plus VAT. Our support is a separate, fixed fee quoted once we know your size and what you already have in place. The Level 2 audit is quoted individually. All prices plus VAT.
