PassCyber / Defence Cyber Certification

Supplying the MOD? Add Defence Cyber Certification to your Cyber Essentials.

The Ministry of Defence expects every supplier in its chain, not just the primes, to hold Defence Cyber Certification Level 0 by 31 December 2026. Level 0 starts with Cyber Essentials. We prepare both together, so one piece of work gets you both.

What Level 0 asks for

Two things: a current Cyber Essentials certificate that covers your business-critical systems, and a completed Level 0 Supplier Assurance Questionnaire assessed against Defence Standard 05-138. Without Cyber Essentials, Level 0 cannot be awarded. DCC becomes a contractual requirement where your contract carries DEFCON 658, and primes are asking their subcontractors to get there now.

Which level you need

There are four levels, 0 to 3, and your MOD delivery team sets yours from the risk profile of the contract. Level 0 is for very low-risk work. Level 1 adds a wider set of controls. Levels 2 and 3 also require Cyber Essentials Plus. Most small suppliers start at Level 0, and you can certify before you hold an MOD contract.

What we do

We confirm your Cyber Essentials scope covers what DCC needs, or take you through Cyber Essentials first. Then we work through the Supplier Assurance Questionnaire with you, close any gaps, and prepare the application. Certification itself is awarded by an IASME-assured DCC certification body, not by PassCyber. Our job is making sure you are ready before you apply.

What it costs

DCC support is an add-on to our Cyber Essentials support and is quoted with it, so you pay once for the shared work. Send us your size and the contract or prime asking for it, and we will come back with a fixed price.
